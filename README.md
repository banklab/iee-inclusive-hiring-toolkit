# IEE Inclusive Hiring Toolkit

Ready-to-use documents behind the fair and inclusive professorial hiring procedure of the
Institute of Ecology and Evolution (IEE), University of Bern, used in two 2025 searches
(Conservation Biology; Mathematical & Computational Ecology).

**Website:** https://banklab.github.io/iee-inclusive-hiring-toolkit/

The files in [`toolkit/`](toolkit) are organised by measure:

- **A** Evaluation criteria & scoring
- **B** Diversity-aware sourcing
- **C** Privilege-aware initial review
- **D** Bias-aware committee process
- **E** Structured interviews
- **F** Inclusive candidate communication

All personal and candidate data has been removed. The completed scouting list and third-party
training materials are not included.

Contact: Prof. Dr. Claudia Bank, Executive Director of the IEE.
